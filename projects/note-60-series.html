PROJECT OVERVIEW

One product family.
Multiple campaign touchpoints.

The NOTE 60 work moved across global campaign adaptation, product storytelling, social platforms, game collaborations and physical event communication. Each part used a consistent visual approach while responding to its own audience and format.

01 / GLOBAL KV & SAUDI LOCALIZATION

Keep the global idea.
Make it local.

The global HQ KV was the starting point. I developed multiple visual options for the Saudi market, then refined the direction into two final executions: POSM and Open Sale.

MY CONTRIBUTION

From global KV
to local campaign system.

Localized the campaign slogan and messaging for the Saudi audience while staying aligned with the HQ visual direction, and designed gifts, pricing and promotional elements to match the KV's aesthetic.

01Explored multiple layout and visual options.
02Adapted the visual language for Saudi retail communication.
03Refined the approved direction into POSM and Open Sale layouts.
02 / PUBG MOBILE · ESPORTS WORLD CUP

One event identity.
A complete social rollout.

The main KV was supplied by PUBG MOBILE. The challenge was to carry its red visual theme across the remaining designs and use it effectively alongside the NOTE Series campaign.

COLLABORATION

Infinix × PUBG MOBILE

The designs were published in collaboration with Infinix across participating markets and on the official PUBG MOBILE page.

01Adapted the supplied event KV into social formats.
02Maintained the campaign's red visual identity.
03Integrated NOTE Series product communication naturally.
03 / NOTE SERIES × BLOOD STRIKE

Product features,
through a gaming lens.

Three social posts translating NOTE Series selling points into gaming-focused communication, pairing product features with the Blood Strike visual universe.

Created in collaboration with Blood Strike and published through its official page.

04 / SAUDI FOOTBALL SOCIAL SERIES

Backing the green.
Supporting Saudi Arabia.

A three-post social series connecting NOTE 60 Pro with Saudi football and the excitement around the tournament, communicating Infinix's support for the Saudi team.

From campaign visuals
to an on-ground experience.

Extended the Infinix NOTE 60 Series campaign beyond digital platforms through a World Cup watch party at Al Masa Café, opposite Kingdom Centre in Riyadh. I handled the event visuals and printed materials, alongside production follow-up and on-site coordination.
